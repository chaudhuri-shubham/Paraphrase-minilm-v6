Minute 1: Introduction & The Business Problem (Referencing Slide 1 - Overview)
(0:00 – 1:00)
"Good morning. I'm representing the Kolkata Royals, and we're addressing a crucial theme: Transforming Customer Experiences and Operational Efficiency.
The reality is, banking is still a solo, transactional journey for many. We store our customers’ money, but we often fail to proactively help them manage it. Our analysis shows four critical gaps:
Isolated Experiences: There's no easy way for partners to track shared goals within the app.
Reactive Service: Our current tools only answer questions when asked; they don't proactively guide users to save or avoid risk.
Lack of Personalization: Customers receive generic promotions that don't match their life stage or specific financial behavior.
No Budgeting Assistance: This forces customers to use third-party apps, fragmenting their financial life."
This leads to the core objective: We must Shift from Reactive to Proactive and Empower Financial Wellness for every customer.
Minute 2: The Solution Architecture & The 5 Layers (Referencing Slide 2 - Methodology)
(1:00 – 2:00)
"Our solution is the HSBC Financial Co-Pilot, a 5-Layer Intelligence Stack built as a modular, API-first overlay on our existing infrastructure.
This stack has a single objective: to deliver personalized guidance. Let's examine the engines:
Layer 1: Transaction Insights Engine: This uses AI and logic to analyze spending patterns and turns raw data into actionable advice. It tells the user: 'You spent too much on groceries; cut back ₹2,000 to reach your goal faster.'
Layer 2: Collaborative Goal Engine: This is our game-changer. It enables secure, transparent tracking for shared financial goals. This drives long-term, sticky loyalty.
Layer 3: Proactive Nudge Engine: This is our behavioral finance tool. It's a real-time rules engine that monitors transactions against goals, triggering contextual interventions to prevent costly mistakes."
Minute 3: The Strategic Value & ROI (Referencing Layers 4 & 5 and Benefits)
(2:00 – 3:00)
"Layers 4 and 5 close the loop on profitability and accountability:
Layer 4: Product Recommendation Engine: Instead of generic noise, we dynamically recommend the right product at the exact moment of need. We recommend the 'HSBC Joint Fixed-Rate Savings Account' or a 'Low-Risk Capital Bond'—not a credit card—because we know their goal is buying a home. The conversion rate on these contextual offers is significantly higher.
Layer 5: Goal Reporting Module: This provides clear, visual reports on goal progress and financial health, maintaining user motivation and engagement.
Our solution provides clear benefits: We deliver Proactive Engagement and Personalization, increase Higher User Engagement, and offer excellent Seamless User Experience."
